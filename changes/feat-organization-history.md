### Added

- **`organizationContext` lists its people's history, not only counts.** `history` holds their messages, mail and
  meetings, newest first, `limit` of them, each with the start of its text and, for a call, its direction, employee and
  length, as a deal context's `communications` does. The `organization_context` schema gains `history`. See
  [business contexts](../docs/dev/BUSINESS-CONTEXT.md).
